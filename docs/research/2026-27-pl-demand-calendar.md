# Premier League 2026/27 — Holiday-Driven Ticket Demand Calendar

**Purpose:** identify which PL fixtures/dates will see the strongest ticket demand, by cross-referencing
all 380 fixtures against the holiday & peak-travel calendars of the markets whose fans travel to England
(UK, US, rich Western Europe, Jewish diaspora, Gulf/Muslim). Home team = who sells the tickets.

**Season window:** 21 Aug 2026 → 30 May 2027. Compiled 2026-06-20.

---

## Methodology — demand = Match Attractiveness × Date Premium

**Match Attractiveness (intrinsic):**
- **Elite (5):** marquee derbies & top-club clashes — North London Derby, Manchester Derby, Merseyside
  Derby, and any pairing of {Arsenal, Chelsea, Liverpool, Man City, Man United, Tottenham, Newcastle}.
- **High (3–4):** a big/global club at home (esp. London clubs — tourists concentrate in London), or a
  London derby involving smaller London sides.
- **Base (1–2):** mid-table / promoted-novelty fixtures (Coventry, Hull, Ipswich, Sunderland, Leeds are
  the 2026/27 novelty sides — a one-off curiosity bump early, not sustained premium).

**Date Premium (holiday/tourism multiplier):**
- **Peak:** Boxing Day → New Year festive block (highest fixture density + peak London tourism + UK schools off).
- **Strong:** a marquee match landing on a bank-holiday weekend or a big source-market travel window.
- **Niche-targeted:** a religious-calendar window that moves a *specific* market (Eid → Gulf; Passover →
  Jewish diaspora, historically Chelsea/Arsenal/Tottenham) onto a London fixture.

Demand for **Omri's tracked teams' HOME games** is called out with ⭐ (his buy targets):
Arsenal (primary), Chelsea, Tottenham, Newcastle, Liverpool, Man City, Man United, Fulham, Brentford,
Crystal Palace, Everton.

## Key caveats for pricing/buying

1. **Kickoff times move.** These fixtures are the most likely to be picked for live TV (and moved to
   Fri/Sun/Mon) — exactly the reschedules the fixtures board's change-detection flags. A move can add or
   kill demand; watch the ⚠️ flag.
2. **Easter is early in 2027** — the Good Friday/Easter Monday weekend has **no PL round** (international
   break). Don't expect an "Easter fixture" premium; it attaches to MW31 (10 Apr).
3. **Passover ≠ Easter this year** — they're separate windows (~3 weeks apart), so the Jewish-diaspora
   demand is its own late-April window, not merged into Easter.
4. **Religious-window demand is niche and directional** — Eid/Passover move a specific market onto
   specific London clubs, not the whole league. Size positions accordingly.
5. **Novelty sides** (Coventry, Hull, Ipswich, Sunderland, Leeds) draw a first-season curiosity bump for
   their *first* visit of a big club, but aren't a sustained premium.
